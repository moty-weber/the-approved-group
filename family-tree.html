Submission Guide

The Golden Rule: Follow the Lineage
You do not need to fill out every frame. Only document the specific branch through which you are claiming European citizenship.

How to Complete the Chart:

  • The Claim Side (EU SIDE): Identify the ancestors who carry your bloodline to Europe. Enter details in the frames labeled (EU SIDE).
  • The Spousal Frames: Enter the spouse of your direct ancestor in the adjacent frame.

Required Information:
Full Legal Name, D.O.B & P.O.B, and Marriage Date.

Expert Note: Accuracy is vital. Ensure all details match your certificates exactly.
