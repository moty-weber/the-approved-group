Submission Guide

The Golden Rule: Follow the Lineage
You do not need to fill out every frame. Only document the specific branch through which you are claiming European citizenship.

Required Information:
Every frame must include Full Legal Name, D.O.B & P.O.B (Birth), and Marriage Date for couples.

Expert Note: Accuracy is vital. Ensure all details match your official certificates.
