Family Tree Submission Guide

The Golden Rule: Follow the Lineage
You do not need to fill out every frame on this chart. You only need to document the specific branch of your family through which you are claiming European citizenship.

How to Complete the Chart:

  • The Claim Side (EU SIDE): Identify the parent, grandparent, and great-grandparent who carry your direct bloodline to Europe. Enter their details in the frames labeled (EU SIDE).
  • The Spousal Frames: Enter the spouse of your direct ancestor in the adjacent frame to complete the couple’s profile.
  • Stop Where the Lineage Ends: If a certain branch is not relevant, leave it blank.

Required Information:
Full Legal Name, D.O.B & P.O.B, and Marriage Date.

Expert Note: Accuracy is our priority. Ensure names and dates match certificates exactly.
