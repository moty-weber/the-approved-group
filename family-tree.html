Family Tree Submission Guide

To ensure your application is processed correctly, please follow these streamlined instructions:

The Golden Rule: Follow the Lineage

You do not need to fill out every frame on this chart. You only need to document the specific branch of your family through which you are claiming European citizenship.

How to Complete the Chart
  • The Claim Side (EU SIDE): Identify the parent, grandparent, and great-grandparent who carry your direct bloodline to Europe. Enter their details in the frames labeled (EU SIDE).
  • The Spousal Frames: In each generation, enter the spouse of your direct ancestor in the adjacent frame to complete the couple’s profile.
  • Stop Where the Lineage Ends: If a certain branch of your family is not relevant to your citizenship claim, you may leave those frames entirely blank.
Required Information

Every completed frame must include official data as it appears on government records:

  • Full Legal Name: As written on birth or marriage records.
  • D.O.B & P.O.B: Date and Place of Birth.
  • Marriage Date: The legal marriage date for that specific couple.
Expert Note: Accuracy is our priority. Ensure all names and dates match your certificates exactly. If any information is missing or unknown, please consult with our team before submitting.
