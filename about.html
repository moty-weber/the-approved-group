The Void We Found

For decades, a significant void existed within our community. Countless families, possessing deep-rooted European heritage, found themselves locked out of their legal rights due to complex bureaucracy, language barriers, and exhausting wait times.

We saw the struggle of the Charedi and Orthodox community—families wishing to reconnect with their ancestral homes in Belgium, Poland, and beyond, but held back by the inability to navigate legal systems. We realized that EU Citizenship is not just a document; it is a key to freedom, security, and heritage.

"Our goal was simple: To globalize the access to European Citizenship, ensuring that no Jew has to stand in endless lines or face rejection when claiming their rightful inheritance."

Legal Restitution & Post-Holocaust Rights

History cannot be rewritten, but justice can be restored. Following the tragic events of the Holocaust, many European nations, spearheaded by the EU, implemented simplified laws for the descendants of those displaced or persecuted.

Today, these Ancestry Rights allow grandchildren and great-grandchildren to reclaim their European passports without the standard residency requirements. We specialize in identifying these unique legal windows, turning family history into a modern-day asset.

The Value of Every Case

There is a common misconception that obtaining a European passport is a simple administrative task. In reality, it is a legal battle for every single case.

At The Approved Group, we sweat over every document. We navigate ancient archives in Antwerp, dig through military records in Poland, and engage with high-level legal chambers. Whether it is securing Student Visas, managing Asylum Rights, or finalizing Marriage-based residency in Belgium, we leave no stone unturned.

We understand that for our clients—from students to large families—every month saved in the process is a month of freedom gained.
