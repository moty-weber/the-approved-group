The Vacuum in the Charedi World

For decades, the global Orthodox community—from the streets of Brooklyn and Monsey to the heart of London and Antwerp—faced a staggering wall of silence when it came to their European legal rights. Families who had lived in Poland, Germany, and Austria for centuries found themselves unable to reclaim their status.

We identified a massive vacuum: a lack of representation that understood the specific needs of the Heimishe community. Whether it's the requirement for valid documentation to visit the resting places of the Chassidic Masters in Kerestir, Lizhensk, or Pshevorsk, or the need for young couples to settle in the diamond district of Antwerp, the need was clear.

The Approved Group was founded to bridge this gap, ensuring that every Yid can navigate the European Union's complex legal landscape with confidence and dignity.

Restitution is a Battle, Not a Process

While some claim that getting an EU passport is a mere administrative formality, the reality is far more intense. It is a legal battle. Nations have created paths for restitution, but the actual implementation is buried under layers of ancient statutes and modern restrictions.

We sweat over every case. We don't just "apply"—we build a comprehensive legal dossier. We work with specialized historians to track names changed during the Holocaust, ensuring that Article 116 of the German Constitution or Polish Citizenship by Descent laws are applied correctly.

Our organization specializes in high-difficulty cases, including Belgium Type D Visas, student residency permits, and family reunification for young married couples. We take the "grill" and the hard work upon ourselves so our clients don't have to.
